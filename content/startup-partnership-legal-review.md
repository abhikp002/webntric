# Webntric Software — Startup Technology Partnership Legal & Governance Review

**Document Status**: Internal Legal & Management Checklist  
**Review Version**: 1.0.0 (October 2026)  
**Notice**: For internal executive, legal, and operational review prior to contract execution. Do not publish as customer-facing terms until reviewed and signed off by qualified corporate legal counsel.

---

## 1. Legal Classification of the Offering
- [ ] **Contractual Characterization**: Determine whether each engagement is formally documented as:
  - (a) A **Selective Technology Services Collaboration Agreement** (Standard initial approach).
  - (b) A **Phased Work-for-Hire with Deferred / Waived Consideration**.
  - (c) A **Strategic Technology Partnership & Incubation Agreement**.
- [ ] Ensure all marketing materials avoid terms like "venture fund", "grant program", or "registered incubator" unless legally incorporated and recognized as such under applicable jurisdiction laws (e.g., Startup India / DPIIT / Section 8 company).

## 2. Equity & Future Ownership Structure
- [ ] In initial phases, no equity or shares are transferred upon application or MVP delivery.
- [ ] If mutual alignment develops into formal partnership:
  - [ ] Require a standalone **Founder / Shareholder Agreement** or **Advisory Shares / Sweat Equity Agreement**.
  - [ ] Explicitly specify vesting schedules (e.g., 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff based on technical milestone delivery rather than mere passage of time).
  - [ ] Verify compliance with Companies Act (India) regarding sweat equity issuance, valuation certificates from a Registered Valuer, and cap-table thresholds.

## 3. Intellectual Property (IP) Allocation & Retention
- [ ] **Founder Background IP**: All existing business plans, customer contacts, domain knowledge, trademarks, and trade secrets remain 100% the property of the founder.
- [ ] **Webntric Background IP**: Webntric retains sole ownership of pre-existing engineering tools, scaffolding, boilerplates, utility libraries, and UI design systems used across projects.
- [ ] **Project-Specific Foreground IP**: Define whether custom code is:
  - (a) Assigned exclusively to the startup upon milestone sign-off, or
  - (b) Licensed to the startup with a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license, with formal assignment conditioned upon agreed future milestones or partnership execution.

## 4. Treatment of Third-Party and Open-Source Software
- [ ] Ensure all third-party libraries (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD) are documented in an open-source manifest.
- [ ] Strictly avoid GPL / AGPL copyleft components in proprietary startup repositories that could force disclosure of the startup's core codebase.
- [ ] Maintain separate founder ownership and billing for third-party SaaS services, APIs, database hosts, and app stores.

## 5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure (NDA) Protocol
- [ ] Initial web application submissions are treated as non-confidential executive summaries.
- [ ] Form explicitly instructs founders: *"Please do not submit confidential trade secrets, source code, passwords, or highly sensitive personal information through this initial form."*
- [ ] Before detailed architecture reviews or sharing of proprietary algorithms, execute Webntric's standard bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA).

## 6. Founder Responsibilities & Non-Technical Obligations
- [ ] Clear contractual separation of roles:
  - Founder retains 100% legal, regulatory, tax, and commercial responsibility for the venture.
  - Founder is solely responsible for user acquisition, sales, customer service, customer data privacy notices, terms of service, and dispute resolution with end users.
  - Founder is responsible for obtaining necessary business licenses, GST registration, payment gateway merchant accounts, and bank accounts.

## 7. Webntric Engineering Responsibilities & Limitations
- [ ] Defined technical sprint milestones with explicit deliverables and acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Scope limitations: clearly state that Webntric does not provide legal, tax, investment, financial, or regulatory compliance advice.
- [ ] No warranty of commercial success, customer adoption, market viability, or third-party investor funding.

## 8. Termination & Exit Rights
- [ ] Unilateral termination for convenience by either party with 14 or 30 days written notice.
- [ ] Termination for cause (breach of confidentiality, failure to participate, fraudulent representations, unlawful activity).
- [ ] Post-termination transition protocol:
  - Handover of code repository, staging assets, and database schemas.
  - Deletion or return of all shared proprietary materials.
  - Preservation of confidentiality and intellectual property clauses post-termination.

## 9. Data Protection, Privacy & Application Retention
- [ ] Application submissions are transmitted over TLS 1.3 (HTTPS).
- [ ] Rate limiting (5 requests per 10 minutes per IP) and honeypot spam traps active.
- [ ] Automated internal email notifications omit full proprietary problem texts from unencrypted transit.
- [ ] Data retention policy: Rejected or non-qualifying applications archived or securely purged after 12 months.
- [ ] Founder rights under DPDP (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, India): right to request deletion of personal information upon written request to info@webntric.com.

## 10. Corporate, Board & Shareholder Approvals
- [ ] Webntric executive director approval required for each accepted partnership project.
- [ ] Startups incorporated as Private Limited or LLP must furnish a board resolution or partner consent authorizing the technical collaboration and IP arrangement prior to development kickoff.

## 11. Dispute Resolution & Jurisdiction
- [ ] Mandatory informal good-faith executive discussion period (30 days).
- [ ] Escalation to binding arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
- [ ] Designated legal seat and exclusive jurisdiction: Courts in Uttar Pradesh / Delhi NCR, India.
